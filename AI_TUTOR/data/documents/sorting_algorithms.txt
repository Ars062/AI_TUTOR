Sorting algorithms arrange elements in a specific order.

Common sorting algorithms:
- Bubble Sort: Repeatedly swaps adjacent elements if out of order
- Merge Sort: Divide and conquer, splits array into halves, sorts, and merges
- Quick Sort: Picks a pivot, partitions around it, recursively sorts
- Insertion Sort: Builds sorted array one element at a time
- Selection Sort: Repeatedly finds minimum element and places it at front

Time complexities:
- Bubble, Insertion, Selection: O(n^2)
- Merge, Quick: O(n log n)

Space complexity:
- In-place sorts: Bubble, Insertion, Selection, Quick
- Out-of-place: Merge Sort (O(n) extra space)
